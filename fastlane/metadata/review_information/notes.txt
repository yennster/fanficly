Fanficly is a privacy-first reader for fiction published on Archive of Our Own (AO3), a large public fanfiction website. No login is required to browse, search, read, follow, or download — you can review the entire app without an account or demo credentials.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT SAFEGUARDS (Guideline 1.2)
- Age gate: a 17+ confirmation is shown on first launch.
- Content filter: Settings > Content & Safety > "Filter mature & explicit works" (ON by default) hides Mature- and Explicit-rated works from all results.
- Hide: the "…" menu on any work hides it from search/browse results (manage under Settings > Hidden works).
- Report: the "…" menu > "Report this work" opens AO3's official abuse-report form. AO3 hosts and moderates all works; Fanficly does not host content.
- Content policy: Settings > Content & Safety > Content policy. We review reports about the app within 24 hours.

PRIVACY
No servers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Nothing about the user leaves the device except requests to archiveofourown.org needed to read/search/log in. The optional AO3 session cookie is stored only in the iOS Keychain.

Fanficly is independent and not affiliated with AO3 or the Organization for Transformative Works.
