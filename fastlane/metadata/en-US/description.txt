Fanficly is a calm, private home for the fanfiction you love.

SEARCH THE WAY YOU THINK
Type "enemies to lovers, slow burn, complete" and Fanficly turns plain English into a precise search. Ships, characters, fandoms, ratings, tags, word count and status all become filters you can tap to refine — no clunky forms.

A READER BUILT FOR LONG NIGHTS
Five reading themes (including Sepia and OLED Black), adjustable fonts, text size, line and paragraph spacing, and column width. Read in a continuous scroll or swipe chapter by chapter. Fanficly remembers exactly where you left off — down to the paragraph.

READ ANYWHERE, EVEN OFFLINE
Save any work to your device and read it with no connection — on the subway, on a plane, anywhere. Export to EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 or HTML to keep your stories in any app you like.

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR WAY
Follow works to be notified when new chapters drop — no account required. Browse by fandom across TV, movies, books, games, music and more. Keep a tidy history of everything you've been reading, plus saved searches and saved filters for the hunts you run often.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Fanficly has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Nothing about you or what you read ever leaves your phone. The only thing stored off-device is your own login session — kept securely in the iOS Keychain, and only if you choose to log in.

YOU'RE IN CONTROL
Filter out Mature and Explicit ratings, hide any work you don't want to see, and report content directly to the source. You must be 17 or older to use Fanficly.

FEATURES
• Natural-language smart search with tappable filters
• Beautiful, fully customizable reader with five themes
• Offline reading and multi-format export (EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, HTML)
• Follow works for new-chapter notifications — no login needed
• Browse by fandom and category
• Saved searches and saved filters
• Recently viewed history
• Optional login to mirror your subscriptions
• 100% private — no tracking, no ads, no servers
