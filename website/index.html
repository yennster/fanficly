I.

Read it your way.

Continuous scroll, chapter by chapter, or page by page like a book. On a wide window the pages pair up into a two-page spread, with the gutter sitting right where a spine would be.

Typography that bends to you

Themes from paper-white to sepia to true black. Five font families, and text size, line spacing, paragraph spacing, kerning and margins you can tune to the point. Save a whole setup as a profile and switch between them.

Your place, to the paragraph

Close the app mid-sentence and come back days later. Fanficly reopens the story where your eyes left it, and your library shows how far you’ve read in everything you’ve started.

Listen when you can’t look

Have any chapter read aloud with the voices already on your device. It follows along paragraph by paragraph, moves on to the next chapter by itself, and on iPhone keeps going from the lock screen.

II.

Search that does what you typed.

Your words go straight to the archive’s own search. As you type, live suggestions from the tag list turn a fandom, ship, character or trope into an exact filter with one tap. Nothing gets guessed behind your back, so teen wolf means Teen Wolf.

  1. Every filter the archive hasRating, warnings, categories, completion, crossovers, word count, language, included and excluded tags, and sort order. Save a filter once, then use it on any fandom.
  2. Browse by mediaAnime, books, movies, TV, video games and more, each with the complete live fandom list and instant search.
  3. Popular, at a glanceThe fandoms, ships and characters with the most works, ranked from the archive’s own counts.

III.

A library that stays put.

Save a work with one tap, star the ones you love, pin the important ones to the top, and sort it all into your own folders. Download stories to read on a plane, or export them as EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF or HTML.

Never miss a chapter

Follow a work or an author right in the app, no account needed, and get a notification when a new chapter or a new work goes up. Tap it and you’re reading.

Your year in reading

Stories finished, hours read, words read and your reading streak, with your top fandoms, ships and authors for any week, month or year. A wrap-up card to share when the year is done.

Your AO3 account, if you want it

Sign in to see your bookmarks and subscriptions and to read and leave comments. It’s entirely optional: everything else works without it.

Private by design

No account with us.
No server. No one watching.

There is no Fanficly account and no Fanficly server. No analytics, no ads, no crash reporters, no tracking of any kind. Your library, reading progress, stats and settings live on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.

When you sign in to AO3, your password goes to AO3 and nowhere else. The only thing kept is AO3’s own session cookie, stored in your device’s secure keychain. The source code is public, so you don’t have to take our word for any of this.
