# Code of Conduct

Fanficly adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## TL;DR

- Be kind, be patient.
- Assume good intent. Ask before assuming bad intent.
- Disagreement is fine; harassment is not.
- This is a fandom-adjacent project. Respect that fandom is a community with its own norms, and that contributors come from many backgrounds.
- Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, or contributions that violate this policy.

## Reporting

Email or open a private issue tagging `@yennster`. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the four-step enforcement ladder in the Contributor Covenant:
1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary ban
4. Permanent ban
